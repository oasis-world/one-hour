# Chess piece artwork

The twelve PNG pieces in this directory are Cburnett's standard chess pieces by Colin M. L. Burnett, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons. They are used unmodified.

- Source files: `Chess_klt60.png`, `Chess_qlt60.png`, `Chess_rlt60.png`, `Chess_blt60.png`, `Chess_nlt60.png`, `Chess_plt60.png` and their `d` (dark) counterparts.
- Source category: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:PNG_chess_pieces/Standard_transparent>
- Example file and author/license record: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chess_plt60.png>
